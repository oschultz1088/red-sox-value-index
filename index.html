Model perspective

This is a simplified comparison of salary and estimated WAR-based value, not a prediction of future performance or a complete measure of a player's contribution.

Methodology & limitations

Estimated value: FanGraphs WAR (fWAR) multiplied by an assumed market rate of $8 million per WAR.

Surplus value: estimated value minus listed 2026 salary. A positive number indicates estimated production value exceeds salary under this model; a negative number indicates salary exceeds estimated value.

Verdict thresholds: undervalued when surplus is greater than $2 million; fairly valued from −$2 million through +$2 million; overvalued when surplus is below −$2 million.

Limitations: WAR is an estimate, the dollar-per-WAR rate is an assumption, salary figures may not capture every contract detail, and a single-season comparison does not account for defense, role, playing time, injuries, age, contract length, team control, or future performance uncertainty. Missing values are not treated as zero.

Sources & data notes

Player salary, FanGraphs WAR, batting average, and ERA values should be documented in the CSV supplied with this site. Verify all figures against current primary or reputable baseball data sources before using the analysis in a formal decision-making context.
